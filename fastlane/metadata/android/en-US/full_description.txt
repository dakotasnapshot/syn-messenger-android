<b>Your conversations. Your choice.</b>

SYN Messenger is a fast, private messaging app built on Matrix, the open standard for secure, decentralized communication.

Stay connected with friends, family, teams, and communities through:

• End-to-end encrypted messages
• Voice and video calls
• Private and public rooms
• Replies, reactions, polls, pinned messages, and threads
• Photo, video, file, and location sharing
• Spaces for organizing your conversations
• Synchronized history across your devices

<b>Choose your server</b>

Sign in with an existing Matrix account, use a public provider such as matrix.org, or connect to a server you control. SYN Messenger works across the open Matrix network, so you are not locked into one provider or one app.

<b>Private by design</b>

SYN Messenger supports end-to-end encryption for private conversations. Your messages remain yours, and you decide where your account and data are hosted.
