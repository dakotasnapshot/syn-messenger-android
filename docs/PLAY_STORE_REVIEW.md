# Google Play review notes

## App access

SYN Messenger is a Matrix client. Most functionality requires a Matrix account.

Use the dedicated synthetic `matrix.org` reviewer account. Enter its username and password only in Google Play Console's **App access** form; do not commit them to this repository or include them in screenshots.

### Reviewer steps

1. Open SYN Messenger.
2. Choose **Sign in**.
3. Select **Change server** and enter `matrix.org` if it is not already selected.
4. Sign in with the reviewer credentials supplied in Play Console.
5. Open the pre-populated fictional rooms to test encrypted messages, replies, reactions, polls, spaces, media, and notifications.

No purchase, subscription, invitation, hardware, or organization membership is required.

## Screenshot privacy

The Play listing uses only fabricated promotional identities and upstream synthetic UI fixtures. It must never contain a real homeserver, account, room, message, customer, employee, or private infrastructure reference.

## Submission boundary

- Complete App content, Data safety, content rating, target audience, and ads declarations before requesting review.
- Keep reviewer credentials in Play Console only.
- Do not publish the SYN website's Google Play button until the public listing URL installs successfully.
- Preserve Element attribution and the AGPLv3 notices in the app and source distribution.
